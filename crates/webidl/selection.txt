# The Web IDL selection of surf's generated DOM: which definitions the binary `bindings` of
# surf-webidl generates into crates/script/src/bindings.rs, and the slice of each of their
# members (story:webidl-bindings, decisions 1 to 6). A member enters or leaves the bindings only
# by a change of this file.
#
# Grammar, one line each, read by surf_webidl::Selection:
#
#   # <text>                                 a comment; an empty line is nothing
#   definitions: <name> <name> ...           the definitions generated, once, before any row
#   global: <name>                           the one of them that is the global object, once
#   <interface> <member> <slice> [<note>]    a row: one per member and one per constructor
#
# <member> is the member's name, `constructor` for a constructor, `iterable` for `iterable<T>`
# (one row of `reading`, not counted as a member). <slice> is one of reading, attributes,
# mutation, selectors, throws, exception. <note> is one or more clauses separated by "; ":
#
#   setter: <slice>        the setter of a writable attribute belongs to that slice
#   reason: <words>        why a member of throws throws; every row of throws has one
#   deviation: <words>     where the member deviates from its standard
#
# A row names an interface of `definitions:` and a member the reader's model holds for it (a
# member of a mixin it includes counts on it); one row per member.
#
# Deviation of every DOMString argument: a lone surrogate reaches the slice as U+FFFD (Rust strings).
